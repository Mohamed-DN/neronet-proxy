# Accessibility

The NeroNet console has not been assessed against WCAG 2.1 AA, with automated tools or
by hand, and no accessibility statement under Italian Law 4/2004 or Directive (EU)
2016/2102 has been made for it.

What exists: the console's design system sets out contrast and focus rules
(`docs/design/`), and the frontend unit tests check some roles and labels. Neither is
an assessment.

An organisation that deploys the console for a public body has to make its own
assessment and publish its own statement through AgID's procedure.
