# NeroNet brand

## The mark

The N of NeroNet is a transit line: up, a 45° diagonal, up again, with a terminus at each end.
The lower terminus is a ring; the upper one is filled with **signal yellow**: "you are here".
It is the same geometry as the network map (0°, 45°, 90°, rings for stations), so the logo and the
product speak one language.

- Small sizes (≤ 24 px): drop the lower ring, thicken the stroke to 7–8, keep the yellow dot.
- App icon: the mark in sheet colour on an ink square (radius 11/48), yellow dot kept.
- Clear space: half the mark's width on every side. Minimum size: 16 px.
- Never: recolour the line, add gradients or shadows, rotate, outline, or move the yellow dot.

## Wordmark

"NeroNet" in Overpass ExtraBold (800), letter-spacing −1%, ink on sheet or sheet on ink. The mark
sits left of the word, its height equal to the cap height × 1.3, gap 0.35 em. The word is one word:
no colour or weight change inside it.

## Colour

Ink `#15232D`, sheet `#F2F4F3`, plate `#FFFFFF`, signal `#F5C211` (one per screen: the yellow
dot, the focus ring, "you are here"), and the six line colours for sub-networks only. Dark: night
`#0E1A22`, plate `#16252F`, ink `#E8EEF0`.

## Voice

Plain, calm, specific; Italian informal "tu". One name per thing (device, gateway, exit,
sub-network, organisation, blocked, data erasure). The transit metaphor lives in pictures and in a
few words (line, stop, interchange), never in puns. Tagline: **"La tua rete, linea per linea."**
(EN: "Your network, line by line.").
